Source of truth for the 1.2 catalogue rebuild. Checked through Wowhead's Forever tooltip API (`https://nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/item/<ID>`, same data as `https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=<ID>`), written "WH" below. Guides: Method's Forever list (https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/list-of-all-consumables-and-enchants-in-wow-forever, beta-era) and Warcraft Tavern Forever alchemy (https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/alchemy/). No Forever per-class consumable guides exist yet (Icy Veins, Wowhead, Maxroll checked); role picks are inferred from effects.

## Endgame picks by role (required level in brackets)

- Strength melee (Warrior, Paladin): Elixir of the Grizzly 250351 (55, +25 Str +2% crit); cheaper Elixir of Ferocity 250350 (55) or Elixir of Greater Strength 9206 (48). Food: Steaming Stag Steak 250071 (45, +20 Str); Mightfish Steak 13934 (45) / Poached Sunscale Salmon 13932 (35), both +40 AP.
- Agility melee (Rogue, Hunter): Elixir of the Mongoose 13452 (30 min); hunters also Elixir of Cunning 250328 (55, +25 Agi +25 Int). Food: Swiftstrike Steak 250072 (45, +20 Agi); cheaper Tender Wolf Steak 18045 (35, +15 Agi).
- Caster: Greater Arcane Elixir 13454, Elixir of the Owl 250337 (55, +25 Int +2% crit), Elixir of Nature Power 250343 (55, +40 Nature). Food: Baked Salmon 13935 (45, +28 spell damage) or Nightfin Soup 13931 (35, +22).
- Healer: Greater Cleric's Elixir 250333 (55, +40 healing), Greater Mageblood Elixir 250341 (55, 20 mp5), Elixir of the Whale 250348 or Elixir of Sages 250338. Drink: Sage's Tea 249870 (45, +44 healing); cheaper Sunny Tea 249869 (35, +33).
- Tank: Elixir of the Phalanx 250329 (55, +400 health +500 armor), Elixir of Greater Fortitude 250335 (55, +400 health). Food: Savory Turtle Stew 250068 (45, +20 Sta).
- Levelling: casters Minor Arcane Elixir 247754 (no level, +5 spell damage), Smoked Sagefish (10, +4 spell dmg), Sagefish Delight (30, +7 spell dmg); strength Big Bear Steak (5, +3 Str), Savory Stag Sliders 250065 (35, +15 Str); Int Roast Raptor 12210 (15, +5 Int), Raptor Rouladen 250076 (35, +15 Int).

## Corrections to the 1.1 catalogue

- Stones: Rough none, Coarse 5, Heavy 15, Solid 25, Dense 35, Elemental 50.
- Oils: Lesser Mana Oil level 40 (10 mp5 +20 healing); Minor Mana Oil 5 mp5 +10 healing; Brilliant Mana Oil 15 mp5 +30 healing; all wizard oils add spell damage **and healing**.
- 21546 is Elixir of Holy Power (+40 Holy, aura 1310077), for Priest/Paladin, not Greater Firepower for Mage.
- Levels: Elixir of Firepower 6373 → 18; Frost Power 17708 → 28; 9206 → 48; Brute Force 13453 → 45; Mageblood 20007 → 40; Minor Agility 2457 and Minor Fortitude 2458 → 2.
- Effects: 9224 is Potion of Demon Slaying (+180 AP); Elixir of Wisdom 3383 is +3 Int; Mongoose lasts 30 min.
- Renames: 2454 Elixir of Minor Strength, 3389 Lesser Defense, 3391 Ogre Strength, 3825 Lesser Fortitude, 8951 Elixir of Defense, 13445 Greater Defense, 9206 Greater Strength, 20007 Mageblood Elixir.
- Foods (Forever level, buff): Herb Baked Egg (none, +1 Sta); Big Bear Steak (5, +3 Str); Smoked Sagefish (10, +4 spell dmg); 12212 is Jungle Stew (35, +15 Agi), not Hot Lion Chops; Sagefish Delight (30, +7 spell dmg); Monster Omelet (35, +15 Sta); Nightfin Soup (35, +22 spell dmg, aura 1249520); Tender Wolf Steak (35, +15 Agi); Grilled Squid (35, +1% crit); Runn Tum Tuber Surprise (35, +15 Int); Smoked Desert Dumplings (45, +20 Str, aura 1248422); Chimaerok Chops (45, +25 Sta). ## Forever-specific notes

- Buff foods also give +5% kill experience (aura 1243969 "Well Fed XP Boost").
- Method (beta): flasks may stack with elixirs; elixirs drop on death, flasks persist.
- Flasks craft at the Alchemy Laboratory, not Scholomance.
- New level 55 elixirs last 30 min.
